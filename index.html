AK

Anjali Kaushik

Teacher Class 4 · EVS · Ghaziabad

Tried a "classroom museum" for the chapter on states of matter — each group set up a small display table and explained their exhibit to visiting classmates. Quietest group of the term ended up asking the most questions. Sharing the exhibit-card template if anyone wants to try it.

#ActivityBased#EVS#Class4
RD

Rakesh Dubey

Principal Kanpur, Uttar Pradesh

We moved staff meetings from "updates" to "one practice, one problem" — every teacher brings one thing that worked and one thing that didn't. Meetings run longer but nobody's staring at their phone anymore. Worth the trade.

#SchoolLeadership#StaffMeetings
NP

Nisha Prasad

Resource Person Teacher Trainer, FLN Mission

Three questioning moves that consistently open up a quiet Class 6–7 classroom: wait 5 full seconds before taking an answer, ask "who can add to that" instead of "who has a different answer," and let one student restate another's point before moving on. Small habits, noticeably more hands up by week two.

#NCF#TeacherTraining#Questioning
MS

Meenal Sharma

Parent Class 2, Noida

Our daughter's teacher started sending home a single "talk about this" question after every EVS class instead of homework. Dinner-table conversation has genuinely improved — she explains what she learned instead of just saying "fine" when we ask about school.
